# Emily Good AI Design System

## Direction

Emily Good AI combines editorial luxury with a precise technology layer. The site should feel warm, capable, and inclusive. Use restraint: one strong idea per section, generous space, and direct copy.

## Palette

- Deep wine `#321523`: primary dark canvas and navigation
- Plum `#4b1f35`: elevated dark surfaces
- Porcelain `#f7f2ed`: primary light canvas
- Blush `#f0ddda`: light action surface
- Rose `#d4a3a5`: interactive accent and focus signal
- Ink `#21141f`: text on light surfaces

Use rose for actions, focus, and small technical details. Do not add competing accent colors.

## Typography

- Display: Georgia or an editorial serif, weight 400, tight tracking
- Interface and body: Arial or Geist, weight 400
- Eyebrows: 10–11px uppercase with wide tracking
- Body: 13–19px with line-height between 1.6 and 1.75

Pair large serif statements with compact sans-serif detail. Keep headlines short enough to leave space around them.

## Layout

- Base structural rhythm: 8px
- Desktop section padding: 112–140px
- Mobile section padding: 80–100px
- Full-bleed dark and light bands divide the story
- Use square cards and fine hairlines. Reserve rounded forms for the clinic app mockup and circular brand geometry.

## Components

- Navigation: translucent wine surface, sticky, 84px high
- Primary action: blush fill, wine text, square corners, minimum 44px target
- Secondary action: transparent with rose hairline
- Service cards: flat surfaces, hairline borders, no drop shadow
- Product imagery: the clinic phone mockup may use shadow to suggest physical depth
- Focus: 2px rose outline with a 5px offset
- Pressed actions: scale to 97%

## Copy

Write in Emily's voice and use first person. Name the deliverable, the user, or the task. Avoid broad claims, filler, business jargon, adverbs, em dashes, and three-part slogans.

## Guardrails

- Preserve the deep plum, porcelain, and rose palette
- Keep the EG/AI placeholder mark until the final logo arrives
- Keep healthcare as a featured product, not the whole brand identity
- Avoid decorative stock photography, excessive gradients, card shadows, and rounded UI everywhere
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and maintain visible keyboard focus
